## Onboarding Process for new employees

1st Form (Pre-Interview)

- Name
- email
- phone number
- DOB
- Nationality
- Citizenship (SG Citizen, SG PR, Foreigner)
- and a lot more info

INTERVIEWER can retrieve data from the form (new tab called candidates)

INTERVIEW HAPPENS -> SHORT LISTED ACCS GO THROUGH, else archive

Email gets sent to employee congratulating and giving them access to their HRMS account with a temporary password

EMPLOYMENT LETTER GETS GENERATED (fully editable + can add new section, default config for department [marketing, retail, logistics, finance, retail operations, HR, design, merchandising, HQ] Signatory)

HR reviews and approves -> signatory personnel to sign -> gets sent to employee

EMPLOYEE REJECTS -> notification tab for signatory personnel and HR

EMPLOYEE ACCEPT AND SIGNS -> their personal folder, notification tab for signatory personnel and HR

2nd Form (Post Employment Letter)

- Upload screenshot Front & Back of NRIC
- Upload screenshot of bank account number & name of the bank
- Upload PDF Entry permit (only if SG PR)
- Date of PR grant (only if SG PR)

## types of employee letters:

- ft retail (retail & retail ops)
- ft hq (remaining departments)
- parttime logistics (3 hourly rates wkday, sat, sun/ph)
- parttime retail (2 hourly rates, wkday & wknd)
- intern reg
- intern sch
